# Privacy Policy — Naarni Service

_Last updated: 1 July 2026_

Naarni Service ("the App") is an internal field-operations tool provided by
Naarni Technologies ("we", "us") to its authorised staff — service engineers,
technicians, depot managers and operations users — for managing vehicle
maintenance job cards. This policy explains what the App collects, why, and how
it is handled.

This App is intended for **authorised business users only**. It is not a
consumer product and requires a valid account provisioned by us.

## Information we collect

| Data | Why we collect it | Shared? |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| **Phone number** | To sign you in via a one-time password (OTP). | No |
| **Name & job role** | To attribute job-card actions and stamp your name on photos. | No |
| **Precise location** | Read **only at the moment you take a photo**, to stamp the place a job photo was captured for field-work verification. Never collected in the background. | No |
| **Photos you capture** | Job-card and vehicle inspection evidence you take inside the App. | No |
| **Device push token (FCM)** | To deliver job assignment and status notifications to your device. | Shared with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging solely to route the notification. |
| **Device identifier** | A random per-install ID used to bind your OTP login to this device for security. | No |

We do **not** collect contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers,
and we do **not** use your data for advertising.

## How your data is used

Collected data is used exclusively to operate the maintenance workflow: signing
you in, recording job-card activity, verifying field work through stamped
photos, and sending you operational notifications. Your session credentials are
stored **encrypted** on your device and are excluded from cloud backups.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties except:

- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging**, only to deliver push notifications.
- Where required by law.

## Data retention & deletion

Job-card records and photos are retained on our servers for as long as needed
for maintenance and audit purposes. To request access to, correction of, or
deletion of your personal data, contact us at the address below and we will act
within a reasonable period, subject to legal retention requirements.

## Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. Session credentials are stored in
Android's encrypted storage (AES-256). Access to backend records is
role-restricted.

## Permissions the App requests

- **Camera** — to capture job and inspection photos.
- **Location (precise, foreground only)** — to stamp photos with capture
  location. Optional; if denied, photos are stamped "Location unavailable".
- **Notifications** — to alert you to job assignments and updates.

## Children

The App is not directed to children and is for authorised staff only.

## Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last
updated" date above.
