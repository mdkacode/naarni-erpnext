# Play Store screenshots — what to upload & what each shows

All images are **1080×1920 (1.78:1)** — within Google Play's rule (max side ≤ 2× min side).
Upload the **`branded/`** set as your phone screenshots (recommended — Naarni badge,
headline + one-line explanation). The plain `final/` set is a no-caption alternative.

## `branded/` — recommended (Naarni-badged, captioned)

| File | Headline | What the image shows |
|------|----------|----------------------|
| `01_home.png` | Your work, front and centre | Home screen — active jobs at a glance + one-tap job creation |
| `02_create_jobcard.png` | Create a job card in seconds | The guided, no-typing New Job Card flow (type + vehicle) |
| `03_profile.png` | Privacy & account control | Profile with Privacy Policy, Terms, and Delete account |
| `04_delete_account.png` | Delete your account, in-app | The account-deletion confirmation (Play compliance) |
| `05_fact_photo.png` | Photo-verified field work | Feature tile — every photo stamped with time/place/user |
| `06_fact_notify.png` | Instant notifications | Feature tile — push/in-app/SMS on assignment & SLA |

## Other Play assets (in `android-app/`)
- `play_store_512.png` — 512×512 app icon
- `play_store_feature_graphic.png` — 1024×500 feature graphic

## Source captures
- `final/` — same 4 screenshots, framed but without the Naarni badge/explanation.
- `01_home.png`, `06_profile.png`, `07_delete_confirm.png`, `08_create_jobcard.png` —
  raw device captures (1080×2424, **2.24:1 — do NOT upload as-is; Play rejects >2:1**).

> To add data-rich screens (Job Card list/detail, stamping camera, Alerts, Fleet),
> capture from an account that has jobs/fleet/depot, then re-run the framing script.
