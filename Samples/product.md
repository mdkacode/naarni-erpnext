**PRD: JOB Card Creation & Assignment**

**Objective:** The Job Card System will be used to digitally create, manage, and track all maintenance and repair tasks for individual buses that arrive at either NaArNi Authorized Service Centers or customer depots.

**Problem Statement:** Below to be solved from the same.

- Manual Job cards are not efficient and analytical.
- Vehicle repair history remains untraceable.
- TAT timelines not able to be tracked for serving or fixing a vehicle.
- Inventory consumption is non trackable.
- No analytical clarity on what has been done.
- Type of JOBs are not monitored ex: PMS/Breakdown/Software Update/Repair
- Job card approval and accountability with respect to Service engineer/depot manager/technician.
- No clarity what feedback is given by the drivers or customer vs what are the issues identified by our team & their fixes.

**Users:** Below will be the users for the tools given their access control will be applicable

| **User Role**    | **Primary Responsibilities**                                       | **System Access**                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Depot Manager    | Managing, assigning, and tracking job cards; inventory allocation  | Create/Edit job cards, assign technicians, approve jobs, allocate inventory                           |
| Service Engineer | Create job cards based on vehicle inspection and customer feedback | Create/update job cards, request inventory, close & reopen the job cards                              |
| Technician       | Execute repair tasks and add newly discovered issues               | Update repair progress, add repair tasks, request inventory                                           |
| Central Ops Team | Monitor open jobs, TAT, and inventory usage across depots          | Dashboard visibility, analytics, reporting                                                            |
| Customer         | To Track the progress of the job card, Rate the job                | To Track the work progress of the job card, Reopen the JOB Card, leaves feedback on the job performed |

**Job Card Type:**

| **Sr** | **Particular**                      | **Application**                                                                                                   |

| 1      | PMS + Repair                        | For the regular maintenance defined at intervals, with solving any other complaints raised by the driver/customer |

**Force Close & Severity Matrix**

When any issue cannot be resolved at the time of job card closure due to inventory not being available or issues get auto-resolved, it must be categorized using the following matrix:

| **Severity** | **Definition**                                | **Force Close Authority**                                                  | **Follow-up Required**                                                            |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Critical     | Bus is non-operational or a safety risk       | N. Maintenance Head                                                        | Within 24 hours - new job card auto-created with Attention to Aftersales Engineer |
| Major        | Bus is operational but with significant issue | N. Depot Manager approval mandatory/Aftersales Engineer Approval Mandatory | Within 24 hours                                                                   |
| Minor        | Cosmetic Issue or Minor Functional Issue      | SE can force close independently                                           | At next scheduled PMS                                                             |

**1.B Score Calculation Method:**

The vehicle health score shall be calculated based on technician inputs captured in the PMS job card. For each component under all categories, the technician shall select one of the following statuses:

- Good
- Repair/Replace Recommended
- Repair/Replace Immediately

Each status shall be mapped to the score as follows:

- Good → 10
- Repair/Replace Recommended → 5
- Repair/Replace Immediately → 0

**PRE-PMS Score:**

- **Category Score Calculation** For the each category, the score shall be calculated based on all component scores within that category.
- **Category Score (%) = (Sum of Component Scores / Maximum Possible Score) × 100**

Where: Maximum Possible Score = (Number of Components × 10)

- **Vehicle Health Score**

The overall vehicle health score shall be calculated as the average of all category scores.

- **Vehicle Health Score (%) = (Sum of Category Scores / Total Number of Categories)**

**Post-PMS Score Update:**

After completion of PMS and/or repair activities, the technician shall update the status of each component. The system shall recalculate component scores based on updated inputs. Updated Category Scores and Vehicle Health Score (Post-PMS) shall be automatically computed.

**Improvement Calculation**

**Improvement (%) = ((Post-PMS Score − Pre-PMS Score) / Pre-PMS Score) × 100**

**System Behavior**

- All calculations shall be performed automatically by the system.
- Scores shall be updated in real-time upon status modification.
- Pre-PMS and Post-PMS scores shall be stored separately for comparison.

**4\. Breakdown**

This will be created when any bus on the trip or at depot standing idle , is not able to perform normal operations for more then 30 mins. The event will be declared as the breakdown.

Below flow to be followed to resolve this
